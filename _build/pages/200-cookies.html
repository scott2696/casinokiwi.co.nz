Cookie Policy

Every cookie this site sets, what it does and how to turn it off. Last updated 1 September 2026.

In plain English

We use three kinds of cookie: ones that make the site work, ones that tell us anonymously which pages get read, and ones that record that you clicked through to a gambling operator so a commission can be attributed. You can decline the optional ones and still read every page in full. We do not use cookies to build an advertising profile of you or to follow you around the web.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store, so it can recognise your browser on a later request. Similar technologies — local storage, pixels, device fingerprints — do comparable jobs, and everything in this policy applies to them too.

Affiliate cookies, explained honestly

When you click a link to an operator, that operator or its affiliate network may set a cookie recording that the visit came from us. If you later open an account, we are credited with the referral and paid a commission. That cookie is set by the operator, not by us, and it tells us nothing personal about you — we receive aggregate referral counts, never your name, email, gambling activity or account details. Declining these cookies does not change anything you can read here; it only prevents commission attribution.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the site; blocking the others will not.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Delete browsing data.
  • Safari (macOS): Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data. On iOS: Settings → Apps → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Private or incognito browsing discards cookies when you close the window. Most browsers also offer a "block all third-party cookies" setting, which switches off affiliate tracking site-wide.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We honour Global Privacy Control signals where your browser sends them, treating them as a request to decline optional cookies.
