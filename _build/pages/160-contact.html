What we cannot help with

  • Your casino account. We do not operate any gambling site and cannot access, unlock, verify or close your account. Contact the operator's support team directly.
  • Bonus claims. Offers are the operator's, and their terms govern. We can tell you what the terms say; we cannot make an operator honour them.
  • Gambling advice. We do not give tips, predictions or systems, and we would treat anyone who does with suspicion.
  • Legal or tax advice. Our law and tax pages are general information. For your circumstances, see a New Zealand lawyer or chartered accountant.
