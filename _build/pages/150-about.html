About CasinoKiwi: Independent Casino Reviews for New Zealand

We built this site because every "best online casino NZ" list we read was written for somebody else — an Australian, a Canadian, a Brit — and then lightly find-and-replaced for New Zealand.

Why we exist

Search "best online casinos NZ" and most of what comes back is a global template with the currency symbol swapped. The payment section lists PayID, which is Australian. The responsible gambling footer points at a British helpline. The legal section either ignores New Zealand law or describes it wrongly. Nobody mentions that POLi barely works any more, that Online EFTPOS exists, that recreational winnings are untaxed here, or that the Department of Internal Affairs is in the middle of licensing an entirely new market.

We are three New Zealanders who thought Kiwi players deserved better than a search-and-replace job. So we test from New Zealand accounts, in New Zealand dollars, against New Zealand law, and we write for people who say "pokies" rather than "slots".

What we actually do

  • We open real accounts and deposit real money. Not a screenshot tour — a funded account, a played session, a requested withdrawal, and a timestamp at every stage.
  • We read the full terms. Every offer we publish has had its conditions read line by line by someone with a law degree, looking for the max bet clause and the cashout cap.
  • We verify licences against the regulator's own register, not against the badge in the site footer.
  • We say when we don't know. Where a figure could not be verified, the page says "check current terms". A confident-sounding invented number is worse than an admitted gap.
  • We publish our method. The scoring model and its weights are public so you can disagree with them.

Our position on responsible gambling

Gambling is entertainment that costs money. For most people that is all it is, and for some it becomes genuinely harmful. We take that seriously in practice, not just in a footer: every site we rank must offer deposit limits, loss limits, reality checks, time-outs and permanent self-exclusion, reachable within three clicks. If those tools are missing or buried, the site does not appear here regardless of what it pays us.

We also do not use urgency language, countdown timers, or the phrase "risk-free" for anything that is not. If you or someone you know is struggling, the Gambling Helpline is free, confidential and available 24/7 on 0800 654 655. Our responsible gambling page lists every New Zealand support service and walks through how to set limits and self-exclude.

How we are funded

We earn commission when readers open accounts through links on this site. That funds the testing programme — real deposits, real withdrawals, real time. It does not buy a ranking position. The order of every list comes from the scores our published model produces, sites we cannot recommend are excluded regardless of commercial terms, and any operator that stops paying players is removed the same week rather than at the next review cycle.
