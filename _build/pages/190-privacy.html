Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information, written to comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Last updated 1 September 2026.

In plain English

We collect very little. If you email us, we hold your email and message so we can reply. If you visit, our analytics records anonymised usage data. We do not sell your personal information, we do not build advertising profiles about you, and we do not receive any personal data back from the gambling operators we link to. You can ask us what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at privacy@casinokiwi.co.nz.

1. Who we are

CasinoKiwi operates casinokiwi.co.nz, an independent New Zealand review site covering online casinos, pokies and betting. For the purposes of the Privacy Act 2020 we are the agency responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Contact: privacy@casinokiwi.co.nz.

We do not collect payment details, identity documents, gambling account credentials or gambling activity data. Never send us those — we will not ask for them, and we will delete them if received.

3. How we collect it

Directly from you when you contact us, and automatically through standard web server logs and privacy-respecting analytics when you browse. We do not buy personal information from third parties and we do not receive personal data from the operators we link to — affiliate tracking tells an operator that a visitor arrived from us, not who you are, and no personal information flows back to us.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use a small number of cookies. Full detail, including how to control them, is in our cookie policy. In summary: strictly necessary cookies keep the site working; analytics cookies (optional) tell us which pages are read; and affiliate tracking cookies record that you clicked through to an operator so a commission can be attributed. You can decline optional cookies without losing access to any content.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who host the site, deliver email or provide analytics, under contract and only as needed to provide those services.
  • Law enforcement or regulators where we are legally required to disclose it.
  • Professional advisers where necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

Some providers are located overseas. Where personal information is transferred outside New Zealand, we take reasonable steps to ensure comparable safeguards apply, consistent with information privacy principle 12.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the editorial inboxes is restricted and protected by multi-factor authentication. We hold as little personal information as possible, for as short a time as possible, which is the most reliable protection there is. No system is perfectly secure, and if a notifiable privacy breach occurs we will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

7. Your rights under the Privacy Act 2020

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you (IPP 6).
  • Request correction of information you believe is inaccurate (IPP 7).
  • Request deletion where we no longer need it.
  • Withdraw consent to optional cookies at any time.
  • Complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner if you are unhappy with how we have handled your information.

Write to privacy@casinokiwi.co.nz. We respond within 20 working days, as the Act requires. There is no charge, and we may ask you to verify your identity before releasing information.

8. Children

This site is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we hold information about a minor, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-party sites

When you follow a link to a gambling operator, you leave this site and their privacy policy governs. We are not responsible for how third parties collect or use your information. Read the operator's privacy policy before creating an account with them.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. Material changes will be flagged prominently.

11. Complaints

Contact us first at privacy@casinokiwi.co.nz and we will try to resolve it. If you remain unsatisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, which provides a free complaints service to New Zealanders.
