The split is deliberate. Rawiri tests the product and the cashier; Maia reads the paperwork and the law. Pages carrying a legal section show that second byline explicitly.

Our editorial standards

  • Named bylines on every page. No anonymous "editorial team" reviews of individual casinos.
  • Separate fact-checking on any page making legal, regulatory or tax claims.
  • Visible dates. Every page shows when it was last updated.
  • Stated uncertainty. Where we could not verify a figure, we say so rather than estimating.
  • Published methodology. Our review process is open, including the scoring weights.
  • Corrections within two working days of a verified error, with the update date changed to reflect it.

If you spot something wrong, write to editor@casinokiwi.co.nz or use our contact form. We would rather be corrected than be wrong.
