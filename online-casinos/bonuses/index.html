The clauses to read before you opt in

Ask one question before claiming

"Is this bonus sticky?" A non-sticky bonus keeps your deposit separate and withdrawable, so the worst case is you lose the bonus. A sticky bonus merges the two, and the worst case is you cannot withdraw anything until the whole requirement clears. The terms page will use the words "cash balance" and "bonus balance" — if they are described as separate, it is non-sticky, and that is the one you want.

Loyalty and VIP schemes, valued honestly

Comp points look generous and usually are not. The number that matters is the exchange rate, and it is rarely on the marketing page.

The 30-second calculation that prices any bonus

Multiply the bonus by the wagering multiple to get your required turnover. Then check whether the multiple applies to the bonus alone or to deposit plus bonus. That is the whole method.

"D+B" means wagering applies to deposit plus bonus; "B" means bonus only. Expected cost is the theoretical house take on the required turnover at 96% RTP — not a prediction of your session.

The four clauses that quietly destroy bonus value

1. Game weighting

Pokies usually count 100% toward wagering. Live blackjack, roulette and baccarat often count 10%, 5% or zero. A bonus advertised at 40x becomes an effective 400x if you play it on 10%-weighted tables. If you are primarily a table or live dealer player, declining the bonus is frequently the better financial decision.

2. Maximum bet while wagering

Typically NZ$5 to NZ$8 per spin or hand. Exceed it even once, even accidentally on a bonus-buy feature, and the operator is entitled to void the bonus and every dollar won from it. This is the most common reason players lose a cleared bonus at the final step.

3. Expiry window

Ranges from three days to thirty. Lucky Vibe's three-day window is the tightest we found among the sites we recommend. Before you claim, divide the required turnover by the days available and ask honestly whether you will play that much.

4. Maximum cashout

Most common on no-deposit and free spins offers. You clear the wagering, you have NZ$800 in winnings, and clause 14 caps your withdrawal at NZ$100. It is not a scam if it is disclosed — but it turns a headline offer into a much smaller one, and it is always in the terms rather than the banner.

Bonus types explained

  • Welcome / deposit match

    The operator matches a percentage of your deposit with bonus credit. Increasingly split across two to five deposits so you commit more than once. Value depends entirely on wagering, not on the headline percentage.

  • Free spins

    A fixed number of spins on a nominated pokie at a fixed stake, usually NZ$0.10 to NZ$0.20. Winnings normally convert to bonus credit with wagering attached, and often carry a max cashout. Check which game — a high-RTP title is worth substantially more than a low one.

  • No deposit bonus

    Credit or spins released without funding an account, usually after email or phone verification. Small in size, heavy in wagering, and almost always capped on cashout. Genuinely useful for testing a site risk-free. See our no deposit casinos page.

  • Reload bonus

    A smaller match offered to existing players, often on a set weekday. Kingdom runs three every week. For a regular player these are usually worth more over a year than any welcome offer.

  • Cashback

    A percentage of net losses returned, often weekly. The best version is cash cashback with no wagering; the common version is bonus credit with wagering attached. Rivo's 25% VIP cashback is the largest on this list.

  • Loyalty / VIP

    Comp points earned on turnover, exchanged for bonus credit, spins or cash. Rooster Bet's Bonus Store is a good example. Worth checking the exchange rate — some programmes need enormous turnover for a trivial reward.

You are allowed to say no

Every site here lets you decline the welcome offer and play with unrestricted cash. Your money is then withdrawable at any time with no turnover, no max bet rule and no expiry. For table players, live dealer players and anyone who plans to deposit and cash out quickly, declining is often the mathematically better choice. A bonus is a tool, not an obligation.

What does 40x wagering mean?

It means the bonus amount must be wagered forty times before a withdrawal is permitted. A NZ$100 bonus at 40x requires NZ$4,000 of turnover. Check whether the multiple applies to the bonus alone or to deposit plus bonus, because the second version roughly doubles the requirement for a matched deposit.

Can I withdraw a casino bonus?

Not the bonus credit itself. You can withdraw winnings generated from it once the wagering requirement is met and any maximum cashout limit is applied. Bonus credit is playing balance, not cash, and unspent bonus credit is removed when you withdraw.

Why did my casino void my bonus?

Most commonly because a single bet exceeded the maximum bet allowed while wagering, typically NZ$5 to NZ$8. Other frequent causes are playing an excluded game, letting the bonus expire, requesting a withdrawal before wagering was complete, or holding a second account. Read the max bet rule before your first spin.

Are no-wagering bonuses real?

Yes, though they are rare and usually smaller than wagered offers. Spino Casino runs a 0x-wagering crypto welcome package, meaning any winnings are withdrawable straight away. Free spins with no wagering also exist at some operators, generally in smaller quantities than heavily-wagered alternatives.

Should I always claim the welcome bonus?

No. If you play mainly live dealer or table games, weighting rules can make a bonus effectively impossible to clear, and declining leaves your deposit fully withdrawable with no max bet rule or expiry. Every site here allows you to opt out at the cashier.

What is a sticky bonus and how do I avoid one?

A sticky bonus merges your deposit with the bonus so nothing can be withdrawn until the entire wagering requirement is cleared. A non-sticky bonus keeps your cash balance separate and withdrawable at any time. The terms page will describe them as separate cash and bonus balances if it is non-sticky — that is the version you want.

Which games clear wagering fastest?

Pokies, because they usually contribute 100%. Crash and instant games contribute 50% to 100% depending on the operator. Roulette and blackjack contribute 5% to 10%, which turns a 40x requirement into an effective 400x, and live dealer games often contribute nothing at all.

Are loyalty and VIP points worth chasing?

The points themselves rarely are. A typical scheme returns under 1% of turnover once you work out the exchange rate. What is genuinely worth having from a VIP tier is faster withdrawals, higher withdrawal ceilings and real cashback — and cashback paid as cash with no wagering is worth several times the same percentage paid as bonus credit.

What counts as bonus abuse?

Holding multiple accounts, exceeding the maximum bet while wagering, playing excluded games, and low-risk betting such as covering both sides of a roulette table to clear turnover cheaply. Operators detect these routinely and the penalty is forfeiture of the bonus and any winnings from it.

Can I withdraw my deposit if I change my mind about a bonus?

With a non-sticky bonus, usually yes — your cash balance stays separate. With a sticky bonus, no, until the wagering clears. Most operators also let you cancel an active bonus from the account area, which forfeits the bonus balance but releases your own funds.

Do bonuses expire and what happens to winnings if they do?

Yes, typically between three and thirty days from claiming. When a bonus expires with wagering outstanding, the bonus balance and any winnings attached to it are removed. Divide the required turnover by the days available before claiming — if the number looks unrealistic, decline the offer.
